## 集成分支实际命令和结果

以下命令从仓库根运行，`$python`、`$godot` 见 ENV；`$tests` 为 development/reports/tests/P0。图形测量与网络容量测量串行，网络绑定 127.0.0.1。每个实验的代码固定在表列提交。

```powershell
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-CONTENT-001/run.py --godot $godot
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-CONTRACTS-001/vectors.py
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-CONTRACTS-002/run.py --godot $godot
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-NAV-001/run.py --godot $godot
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-BATTLE-001/run.py --godot $godot --count 500 --out "$tests/integration-battle-500"
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-BATTLE-001/run.py --godot $godot --count 1000 --out "$tests/integration-battle-1000"
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-NET-001/run.py --godot $godot --output "$tests/integration-net-001.json"
& $python test_scenarios/p0/P0-NET-002/run.py --godot $godot --output "$tests/integration-net-002.json"
```
